Glossary

  • Verdict — GREEN means ready by the rules, YELLOW means look again, RED means do not release yet.
  • Pass rate — passed tests divided by passed plus failed tests, as a percentage. Skipped tests are not counted.
  • Flaky test — a test that passes sometimes and fails sometimes without any code change.
  • Seen before — a similar past failure from the vector memory, shown for context only.
  • Confidence — how sure the classifier was, from High to Low. Low confidence sends the failure to investigation first.
